NanGuo Pinyin is a Simplified Chinese educational typeface that embeds Hanyu Pinyin pronunciation guides directly above each character as font-native ruby text — no HTML markup required. Based on Noto Sans SC, the annotation is encoded in the glyph itself as a TrueType composite, making it universally compatible with any application that can render text.

The family ships as six numbered variants corresponding to the six possible tonal readings of Chinese characters. NanGuo Pinyin 1 displays the primary pronunciation for all 6,763 GB2312 Simplified Chinese characters. NanGuo Pinyin 2 shows the secondary reading for the 2,381 multi-pronunciation characters (多音字), while characters with only one reading render without annotation. Variants 3 through 6 follow the same pattern for tertiary and rarer readings.

Typical use: set body text in NanGuo Pinyin 1 to annotate all characters; overlay a second text run in NanGuo Pinyin 2 to expose alternate readings for study. The font is particularly suited to language-learning materials, digital dictionaries, children's books, and educational publishing where inline phonetic guidance is essential.

Coverage: 6,763 GB2312 Simplified Chinese characters with full Pinyin annotation · 1,349 pre-composed Pinyin syllable glyphs · 20,976 CJK Unified Ideographs · Hiragana, Katakana, Bopomofo · Latin Extended (full Pinyin diacritic set) · Complete Noto Sans SC Latin/punctuation coverage.
